Japan rewards trips that mix **one main leisure focus** with easy side days. Use JPFun to choose **ski, scuba, surf, or camp**, filter by region, then bolt on bowls, baths, or a round of golf when the weather flips.

## Pick the season first

- **Dec–Mar:** Ski is the default. Hokkaido for powder; Hakuba / Yuzawa for Tokyo access.
- **Jun–Sep:** Scuba and surf lead. Okinawa for clear water; Shonan / Chiba for weekend waves.
- **Shoulder months:** Camp around Fuji / Motosu, or chase autumn swells.

Open the matching map on JPFun — **Ski**, **Scuba**, **Surf**, or **Camp** — and narrow by region before you book lodging.

## One primary, one backup

- Storm day on the boat → switch to a city food day or an onsen soak.
- Flat surf week → try a pool session or a short camp overnight.
- Ski whiteout → keep the trip fun with ramen and a hot-spring town.

## Easy add-ons (same trip energy)

- **Ramen** after cold or long days — shop map: [okramen.net](https://okramen.net/)
- **Onsen / ryokan** to recover — [okonsen.net](https://okonsen.net/)
- **Golf** on dry, scenic days — [okcaddie.net](https://okcaddie.net/)

You do not need a second “project.” Treat these as next pins on the same itinerary.

## A simple planning sequence

1. Choose leisure on JPFun and shortlist 2–3 spots on the map.
2. Read the activity guides (ski vs ski, scuba vs scuba).
3. Book the base stay near your first spot.
4. Leave one flexible day for food, onsen, or golf.

That is enough structure for a first Japan leisure trip without a binder full of tabs.
