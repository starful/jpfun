**Lake Motosu** is the classic Fuji camp postcard. Treat it as a **one-night route** with a weather-honest morning, not a three-lake marathon.

Pin Motosu-area sites on the JPFun **Camp · Chubu / Fuji** map, then book before the clear-sky forecast goes viral.

## Day 0 — Drive & pitch

- Leave Tokyo with Friday PM traffic in mind, or start early Saturday.
- Pitch before dark; Motosu wind can punish late setups.
- Keep dinner simple — stove, prepped food, or a reserved nearby meal.

## Day 1 — Dawn + exit

- Wake for calm water and Fuji reflections when the forecast cooperates.
- Break camp by checkout; do not squeeze a second lake “just because.”
- **Soft exit options:** onsen rinse on the Tokyo-bound corridor ([okonsen.net](https://okonsen.net/)), or a long lunch stop. Ramen maps help on the way home ([okramen.net](https://okramen.net/)).

## Gear reality check

- Car-camp kit or glamping booking — decide before you leave the city.
- Extra pegs, wind-rated setup, warm layer even in summer nights.
- Pack out trash; lake edges fill fast on bluebird weekends.

## Pairing note

Winter powder belongs on the Hokkaido or Hakuba ski routes — flip seasons, do not force ski + Motosu into one weekend. Golf can wait for a dry inland day via [okcaddie.net](https://okcaddie.net/) on a different trip.
