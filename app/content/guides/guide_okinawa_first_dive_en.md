Okinawa is one of the easiest places in Japan to try **clear-water boat diving**. Most visitors base in Naha and day-trip to the **Kerama Islands**; Ishigaki is a separate hop for manta-focused trips.

Use the JPFun **Scuba** map to compare dive areas, then lock lodging near your departure pier.

## Before you fly

- Confirm your **C-card** (OW or higher) and log book. Many boats ask for recent dives or a check dive.
- Book **hotel + dive shop** early for Golden Week, Obon, and consecutive holidays.
- If you get seasick, pack medication and eat light the morning of the boat.
- Rent gear your first days; buy only what you already know you like.

## Kerama day-boat rhythm

- Meet early at the shop or Tomari port, then 30–60 minutes to the sites.
- Typical day: two dives, lunch on board, back mid-afternoon.
- Visibility and current change fast — trust the guide’s site call.
- Surface intervals are for water, shade, and not scrolling in the sun.

## Shop questions worth asking

- English or Korean briefing available?
- Maximum group size per guide?
- Nitrox / computer rental?
- Bad-weather policy and refund window?

## After the dive

- Easy dinner near Kokusai-dori; skip heavy drinking the same night if you dive again tomorrow.
- For a flat day, switch to the JPFun **Surf** map (Okinawa) or plan a rest day inland.
