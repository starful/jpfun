Tokyo makes surfing easy to attempt and easy to botch. The fix is a **single coast**, a realistic swell check, and a plan B that is still fun.

Open JPFun **Surf · Kanto**, then choose **Shonan** (train-simple) or **Chiba** (often cleaner peaks, longer transit).

## Day 0 — Friday night or early Saturday

- Check swell, wind, and tide the evening before — do not wake up undecided.
- Rent boards near the break if you are not traveling with gear.
- Stay one train stop off the absolute densest beach strip if you want sleep.

## Day 1 — Surf the window

- Morning sessions usually beat afternoon sea breeze.
- Two short sessions beat one exhausted marathon if you are weekend-rusty.
- Midday: shade, water, and a simple lunch — save the big ramen hunt for after you rinse.

## Day 2 — Peak or pivot

- **Still firing:** second morning, then return to Tokyo with buffer.
- **Flat / blown out:** switch to a city food day ([okramen.net](https://okramen.net/)) or a short onsen escape inland ([okonsen.net](https://okonsen.net/)) instead of forcing mush.
- Optional dry-day add-on on a longer trip: a round via [okcaddie.net](https://okcaddie.net/) — not required for a pure surf weekend.

## Crowd & etiquette notes

- Summer weekends are busy; give space and follow local priority.
- Pin JPFun spots for parking / station context, then ask a local shop which peak is working that morning.

## Route rule

Do not combine Shonan **and** Chiba in one weekend. Pick one, surf it well, and leave the other coast for next time.
