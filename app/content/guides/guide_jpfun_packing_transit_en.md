A Japan leisure trip fails more often from **wrong layers and tight transfers** than from picking the “wrong” mountain.

## Shared packing core

- Passport, cards, small cash, offline maps / JPFun shortlist screenshots.
- Rain shell, quick-dry mid layer, comfortable walk shoes.
- Medicine, power bank, universal plug.

## Activity add-ons

- **Ski:** goggle spare, warm gloves, insurance details.
- **Scuba:** cert card photos, logbook, reef-safe sunscreen, seasickness plan.
- **Surf:** wax/comb if needed, rash guard, coin for showers.
- **Camp:** headlamp, trash bags, wind stakes, warm night layer.

## Transit habits

- Build 30–60 minutes of slack on winter roads and island boats.
- One primary base per weekend — do not “collect” regions.
- Soft days (ramen / onsen / golf) live on peer maps: [okramen.net](https://okramen.net/), [okonsen.net](https://okonsen.net/), [okcaddie.net](https://okcaddie.net/).
