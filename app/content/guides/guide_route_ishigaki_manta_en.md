**Ishigaki** rewards divers who treat the **manta / channel boat** as the itinerary, not a side quest.

Use JPFun **Scuba · Okinawa** pins for geography, then book a Yaeyama operator that matches season and experience.

## Day 0 — Island settle

- Fly into Ishigaki; confirm tomorrow’s briefing and seasickness plan.
- Early sleep — channel mornings start early.

## Day 1 — Primary boat

- Trust the shop’s site rotation; mantas are not guaranteed every tank.
- Log conservatively; save energy for day 2 if conditions stack.

## Day 2 — Second chance or island soft day

- Another boat if weather and nitrogen plan allow.
- Cancel day: island food, light beach — not a forced shore dive in surge.

## Day 3 — Fly buffer

- Respect no-fly intervals. Build the route so you are not racing the airport.

## Route rule

Do not mix Ishigaki and a Kerama day-boat from Naha in one short trip — pick one island plan.
