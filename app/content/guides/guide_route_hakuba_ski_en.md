**Hakuba** is the Honshu powder/alpine hybrid: closer than Hokkaido for many Tokyo flights, with multiple hills in one valley.

Open JPFun **Ski · Nagano**, pin Hakuba-area resorts, and book a village stay you can bus between.

## Day 0 — Arrive

- Fly/train toward Nagano; bus into Hakuba.
- Rent gear in the village; confirm which hill opens earliest tomorrow.

## Days 1–2 — Ski the valley

- Day 1: home hill only — learn snow report and lift wind holds.
- Day 2: second resort in the same valley if legs and weather allow.
- Do not day-trip to Hokkaido logistics; keep the radius tight.

## Day 3 — Flex / exit

- Extra ski morning **or** onsen recovery ([okonsen.net](https://okonsen.net/)) before the Tokyo transfer.
- Ramen stop on the way out ([okramen.net](https://okramen.net/)).

## Route rule

Hakuba **or** Niseko on a first trip — not both. Save Hokkaido for a dedicated powder route.
