**Izu / Chubu** diving suits Tokyo-based schedules: overnight bus or train, dive, return.

Open JPFun **Scuba · Izu / Chubu**, shortlist shops that match your cert and last-dive date.

## Day 0 — Travel south

- Arrive before shop briefing; confirm entry points and parking.
- Pack reef-safe sunscreen and a warm layer for boat wind.

## Day 1 — Dive day

- Shore circuits or a short local boat — follow the shop’s site call.
- Two tanks is a solid weekend goal; do not stack night dives unless planned.

## Day 2 — Buffer

- Optional easy second dive **or** scenic drive back.
- Fly-international rules matter less here, but still rest if you feel drained.

## Route rule

Izu is **not** a substitute for Kerama visibility dreams — pick Okinawa when water clarity is the whole point.

## Who This Suits

Best for certified divers based in Tokyo who want a low-hassle weekend: short travel, shore or short-boat sites, no flight stress. Good for stacking bottom time between bigger trips, or testing gear before a longer Okinawa run.

**Quick tips**
- Check tide and surge reports before shore entries — conditions shift fast on exposed coastlines.
- Pack a spare mask strap and O-ring kit; small parts are easy to forget on a quick weekend trip.
- Confirm your shop's minimum group size — some Izu operators need a few bookings before the boat runs.

## Izu Diving Weekend FAQ

**How far is Izu from Tokyo?** Most shops are reachable by train or overnight bus, making this a realistic two-day trip without flights.

**Do I need advanced certification?** No — Izu suits open-water divers as well as advanced divers looking for easy shore or short-boat sites.

**Best season?** Shops run dives most of the year; conditions vary by site, so confirm current visibility and surge with your operator when booking.

**Gear to bring?** Reef-safe sunscreen, a warm layer for boat wind, and a spare mask strap or O-ring kit for a smooth weekend.
