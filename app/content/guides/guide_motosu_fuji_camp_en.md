**Lake Motosu** is the postcard Fuji camp: reflection photos at dawn, car-camp convenience, and easy day trips around the Fuji Five Lakes. It is popular for a reason — book early and plan for wind.

Pin Motosu-area camps on the JPFun **Camp** map (Chubu / Fuji), then decide tent vs. glamping based on how much gear you want to haul.

## Booking & site types

- Peak: Golden Week, Obon, autumn foliage weekends, and clear-weather Fuji forecasts.
- Auto sites vs. walk-in lawns — check vehicle length and ground clearance rules.
- Glamping is worth it for first-timers without full kits; DIY is cheaper if you already own a car-camp setup.
- Read cancellation windows; mountain weather cancels plans often.

## Light & wind

- Reflections are best on calm, clear mornings — arrive the evening before when you can.
- Afternoon winds off Fuji can flatten flimsy tents; bring solid pegs and a low profile.
- Cloud cover ruins the “perfect shot” more often than tourists expect — enjoy the lake anyway.

## Pack list (car-camp)

- Low chair, table, stove, headlamp, warm layer even in summer nights.
- Cash for some farm stands and coin showers.
- Trash bags — pack out what you pack in.
- Printed or offline map; signal can dip in folds of the lakeshore.

## Pair with the rest of the trip

- Summer Motosu → winter flip to the JPFun **Ski** map (Nagano / Hakuba) for the same mountain energy.
- Want a rinse after dusty camp days? Look for onsen towns on the drive back toward Tokyo.
