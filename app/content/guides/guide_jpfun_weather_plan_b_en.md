Weather is not a plot twist in Japan outdoor travel — it is part of the calendar.

## Decide by 10:00–11:00

- Check the activity you booked (ski lifts, boat SMS, surf wind).
- Call the day early enough to still reach an onsen or city food window.

## Soft-day menu

- **Onsen / ryokan day bath** — [okonsen.net](https://okonsen.net/)
- **Ramen / comfort food** — [okramen.net](https://okramen.net/)
- **Scenic dry golf** (if you packed for it) — [okcaddie.net](https://okcaddie.net/)
- Museum / station-area walk near your base — zero FOMO required.

## What not to do

- Do not invent a brand-new region three hours away.
- Do not force a second high-risk session in the same storm.

Keep JPFun as the **primary pin map**; use soft days to protect the overall mood of the trip.
