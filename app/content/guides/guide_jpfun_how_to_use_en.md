JPFun is intentionally **not** one giant mixed map. That keeps decisions clean.

## The flow

1. On the home page, choose an activity tile (or golf / onsen outbound links).
2. On the activity map, filter by **region**.
3. Shortlist 2–3 pins — not fifteen.
4. Read a matching **Recommended route** or activity guide.
5. Book lodging near pin #1; keep pin #2 as weather backup.

## Language & guides

- Switch EN / KO in the header.
- **JPFun guides** on the home page cover seasons, packing, budget, and plan B.
- Activity pages hold ski-only / dive-only detail guides.

## What JPFun is not

- Not a live snow or swell forecast.
- Not a booking engine for every boat and campsite.
- It is the **decision map** before you commit money.

If you only do one thing: pick the activity, then the region — never the reverse on a first trip.
