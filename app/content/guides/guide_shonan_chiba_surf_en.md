You do not need a car for a solid **Tokyo-area surf weekend**. **Shonan (Kugenuma)** is the classic train-friendly beach break; **Chiba (Hebara / Isumi)** rewards the extra travel with more consistent Pacific swell.

Open the JPFun **Surf** map (Kanto) to pin spots, then match lodging to the last train home — or stay one night on the coast.

## Shonan in one day

- Odakyu to Fujisawa / Katase-Enoshima, then a short walk or bus to the sand.
- Best for beginners and soft beach peaks; expect crowds on sunny weekends.
- Rent boards near the beach; lockers fill early on holiday mornings.
- Check wind: onshore afternoons can close out the fun.

## Chiba when you want real swell

- Longer ride (train + local bus/taxi, or rental car).
- Pacific-facing beaches hold size better after typhoon swells — respect local priority.
- Intermediate comfort helps; shorebreak and rips are real after big days.
- Pack cash; some lots and shops are still cash-first.

## Timing tips

- Arrive at first light on Saturdays — parking and peaks are friendlier.
- Midday summer heat: hydrate and use reef-safe sunscreen.
- Flat week? Switch to the JPFun **Scuba** map or plan a pool session.

## Etiquette that keeps you welcome

- Do not drop in. Paddle wide. Share peaks.
- Keep soft-tops and lessons out of the critical takeoff zone when locals are lined up.
- Pack out trash; beach towns notice.
