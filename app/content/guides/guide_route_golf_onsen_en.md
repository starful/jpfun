Not every JPFun trip needs powder or swell. A **golf + onsen** overnight resets legs and mood between bigger activity weekends.

## Day 0 — Round + check-in

- Book the tee time first on [okcaddie.net](https://okcaddie.net/), then place lodging within a sane drive.
- Pack light: soft spikes, rain shell, cash for caddie/cart norms as required.

## Day 1 — Soak & exit

- Morning onsen or breakfast ryokan pace via [okonsen.net](https://okonsen.net/).
- Optional ramen on the highway home ([okramen.net](https://okramen.net/)).

## Route rule

One round, one soak. Do not stack a second 36-hole grind onto a recovery trip.
