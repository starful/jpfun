Western Honshu camping is about **bridges, coasts, and unhurried nights** — not Fuji postcard pressure.

Pin sites on JPFun **Camp · Chugoku / Shimanami**, book one overnight, and keep the radius human-sized.

## Day 0 — Arrive & pitch

- Reach your site with daylight for setup and a short walk or ride.
- Wind off the Seto can surprise; stake well.

## Day 1 — Slow morning + exit

- Coffee with the view; do not force a second campsite “collection.”
- Exit via a bath stop ([okonsen.net](https://okonsen.net/)) if you are driving east.

## Route rule

This pairs poorly with a same-weekend Motosu dash. Pick west **or** Fuji.
