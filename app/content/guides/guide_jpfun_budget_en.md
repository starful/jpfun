Budget stress usually comes from ignoring the **big three**: transport, lodging, and the activity ticket (lift / boat / rental / campsite).

## Lock these first

1. Flights or long-distance rail.
2. Base lodging near your JPFun shortlist.
3. Lift pass, dive boat, board rental, or camp booking.

## Flexible bucket

- Meals (mix konbini + one nice bowl).
- Onsen day baths.
- Souvenirs.

## Activity ranges (rough mental model)

- **Ski:** passes + rental dominate; powder towns cost more on weekends.
- **Scuba:** boats are the line item; do not cheap out on operators.
- **Surf:** transit + rental; lodging can be hostels.
- **Camp:** site fees look small until parking, gear, and food add up.

Leave a **10–15% buffer** for weather pivots (extra night, taxi, second boat attempt). That buffer is cheaper than a ruined mood.
