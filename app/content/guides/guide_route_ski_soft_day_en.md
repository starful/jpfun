Whiteouts happen. The win is staying in the mountains with a **soft-day script** instead of sulking in the hotel.

## Morning decision

- Check lift status on your JPFun ski shortlist (Nagano or Niigata).
- If upper lifts are held, ski lower terrain briefly **or** call the ski day by 11:00.

## Afternoon soft circuit

- Ramen map: [okramen.net](https://okramen.net/)
- Onsen / ryokan day bath: [okonsen.net](https://okonsen.net/)
- Keep travel under 60–90 minutes from your base.

## Next morning

- Re-check snow; resume the original ski route if the mountain opens.

## Route rule

Soft days support a ski trip — they are not an excuse to invent a new city itinerary from scratch.
