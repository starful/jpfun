**Yuzawa / Niigata** is built for people who need powder without a domestic flight.

Pin resorts on JPFun **Ski · Niigata**, stay walking or short-bus distance from the station area.

## Day 0 — Shinkansen in

- Tokyo → Echigo-Yuzawa; check last buses to your hotel.
- Rent early Saturday if shops close early Sunday.

## Day 1 — Main ski day

- Ski the closest reliable hill first.
- Watch avalanche and wind notes; stick to marked runs on a short trip.

## Day 2 — Second session or pivot

- Morning ski, then return with buffer.
- Flat snow / ice: town food day ([okramen.net](https://okramen.net/)) or a quick onsen ([okonsen.net](https://okonsen.net/)).

## Route rule

This is a **48–60 hour** route. Do not bolt Hakuba onto the same weekend.
