Scuba weekends in Okinawa fail when lodging comes before the **boat booking**. Reverse it: lock the dive day, then place the hotel.

Start on the JPFun **Scuba · Okinawa** map and shortlist shops that match your certification level.

## Day 0 — Fly & brief

- Arrive Naha (Kerama / west coast day boats) or Ishigaki (Yaeyama / manta season).
- Evening gear check and shop brief; confirm pickup time and seasickness plan.
- Light dinner; heavy drinking the night before a boat is a bad trade.

## Day 1 — Boat day (the core)

- Two or three tanks is enough for a first Okinawa boat day.
- Follow the shop’s surface-interval plan; do not freestyle extra shore dives the same afternoon unless they say so.
- Save phone photos for the ride back — enjoy the water first.

## Day 2 — Buffer or second dive

- **Great visibility:** optional second boat or an easy shore dive if you are cleared.
- **Wind / cancel:** beach walk, island food, or a recovery soak if you extend north — keep [okonsen.net](https://okonsen.net/) as a soft idea for a longer trip, not a must on a 48-hour dash.
- Fly-home buffer: most operators want 18–24 hours before flying — build that into the route.

## Booking checklist

- Certification cards + logbook photos ready.
- Tell the shop your last dive date; refreshers beat rusty pride.
- Pin JPFun dive sites only as geography — the operator chooses sites for the day’s sea.

## Who this route is for

Certified divers who want **one excellent water day** more than a packed sightseeing checklist. Add Tokyo surf or Fuji camp on a different weekend — do not glue them onto this flight.

## Quick planning notes

- First Okinawa dive trip? Ask your shop whether Kerama (calmer, closer to Naha) or Ishigaki (Yaeyama currents, manta season) fits your comfort level before booking.
- Traveling with a non-diving partner? Pair this route with a separate Naha or Ishigaki sightseeing day instead of squeezing them onto the boat schedule.
- Solo certified divers can usually join a shop's mixed-group boat without chartering a private trip — just ask when you book.

## Who This Okinawa Dive Weekend Suits

Certified divers with a few logged dives who'd rather lock in one strong boat day than chase a packed sightseeing list. Have your c-card, logbook, and dive insurance info ready — most Kerama and Ishigaki shops ask before you board. New to Okinawa diving or chasing manta season at Ishigaki? Message the shop directly about current conditions before you lock flights, since the operator — not this route — picks the day's actual dive sites.
